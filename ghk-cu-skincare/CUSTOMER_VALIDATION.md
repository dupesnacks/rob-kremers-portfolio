### Questions

(Placeholder; to be refined before Phase 10 launch)

1. **Awareness:** Have you heard of GHK-Cu or copper peptides in skincare?
2. **Interest:** Would you try a premium cosmetic serum with GHK-Cu?
3. **Price:** What's your price comfort for a serum like this? ($49, $59, $69?)
4. **Repeat:** If you liked it, would you buy again? How often?
5. **Differentiation:** What would make this different from other peptide serums?
6. **Claims:** What claims would matter to you? (appearance, feel, results timeline?)
7. **Trust:** What would make you trust the brand? (Ingredients? Reviews? Education?)
