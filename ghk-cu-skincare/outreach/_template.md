## Email Draft

**Subject:** GHK-Cu Cosmetic Serum Contract Manufacturing Inquiry

**To:** [Supplier contact from SUPPLIERS.md]

---

Hi [First Name],

I'm Rob Kremers, founder of [Brand Name TBD], and we're exploring U.S. contract manufacturing for a premium cosmetic serum featuring GHK-Cu (Copper Tripeptide-1) as the active ingredient. We're targeting a lean MVP launch with high quality standards and positioned strictly as a cosmetic product (not a drug or therapeutic claim).

I'm reaching out to a select group of established manufacturers to understand capability and fit.

**A few quick questions:**

1. **GHK-Cu Experience:** Have you formulated or produced cosmetic products containing GHK-Cu? If so, how many clients and in what formats (serum, cream, spray)?

2. **Documentation & Testing:** Do you provide:
   - Certificate of Analysis (COA) from 3rd-party testing labs?
   - Batch records with ingredient sourcing dates and traceability?
   - Stability testing data?
   - Microbial testing (sterility)?

3. **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):** What's your typical MOQ for a cosmetic serum in the $50–$70 retail range (likely 30–50 mL bottles)? Are there volume discounts?

4. **Lead Time:** From order placement to delivery, how many weeks do you typically need?

5. **Pricing:** Ballpark COGS for a peptide-infused hydrating serum at your MOQ? (We're targeting $6–$12/unit before packaging + fulfillment.)

6. **Certifications:** Are you FDA-registered? GMP-certified? ISO-certified? Any organic/vegan/cruelty-free certifications?

7. **Claims Compliance:** Are you familiar with FTC/FDA distinctions for cosmetic vs. drug claims? Can you advise on substantiation pathways for cosmetic claims like "improves appearance of fine lines" and "moisturizes complexion"?

8. **References:** Are you willing to share references from prior cosmetic brands you've worked with? (Especially if they've used active peptides.)

**Timeline:** We're evaluating suppliers now (June) and aiming to have samples + final quotes by end of month. If we're a fit, we'd move to sample production by early July.

No commitment at this stage — just exploratory.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best,
Rob Kremers
