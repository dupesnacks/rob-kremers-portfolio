## Scoring Guide

- **1** = High risk / likely blocker
- **2** = Concerning gaps
- **3** = Neutral / some risk, some upside
- **4** = Strong position
- **5** = Excellent / minimal risk

**Interpretation:**
- **45+** = Strong go signal
- **38-44** = Deeper diligence needed
- **30-37** = Validate more before committing
- **20-29** = Weak position; pivot or no-go
- **<20** = No-go

**Hard Stop:** Legal defensibility must be ≥4/5 regardless of total score. Any score below 4 on legal defensibility triggers immediate escalation in `DECISIONS.md`.
