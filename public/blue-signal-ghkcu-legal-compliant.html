🛡️ Legal Compliance Framework

CRITICAL: All messaging below adheres to FTC/FDA regulations for cosmetic skincare products. GHK-Cu is positioned as a cosmetic ingredient, not a drug. This means:

  • ✅ Claims focus on appearance (how skin looks)
  • ✅ NO therapeutic language (treat, heal, cure, repair, regenerate)
  • ✅ All claims substantiated with clinical studies or published research
  • ✅ Explicit medical disclaimers on all marketing
  • ✅ Influencer disclosures (#ad) on all sponsored content
  • ✅ Product liability insurance required before launch
⚠️ Golden Rule: If a claim sounds therapeutic (treats, heals, cures, reduces inflammation, stimulates collagen production), IT'S A DRUG CLAIM. Reframe it as appearance-focused or cut it entirely.

📋 Claim Translation Guide: Drug Language → Cosmetic Language

Here's how to convert scientific benefits into legal cosmetic claims:

❌ NOT ALLOWED (Drug Claims) ✅ ALLOWED (Cosmetic Claims) Substantiation Required
"Stimulates collagen production" "Helps skin appear firmer" / "Promotes a more youthful-looking appearance" Clinical study measuring skin firmness/elasticity
"Heals damaged skin" "Helps improve the appearance of skin texture" / "Supports healthy-looking skin" Clinical study on texture/smoothness improvement
"Treats acne" "May help reduce the appearance of blemishes" / "Promotes clearer-looking skin" Clinical study on blemish appearance reduction
"Reduces inflammation" "Helps skin appear calmer" / "Promotes a more even-looking complexion" Clinical study on redness/appearance improvement
"Repairs skin barrier" "Helps skin look and feel more resilient" / "Supports skin's natural protective layer appearance" Clinical study on skin barrier function (appearance)
"Regenerates skin cells" "Promotes healthy-looking skin" / "Helps maintain the appearance of youthful skin" Clinical study on skin appearance/quality improvement
"Prevents wrinkles" "Helps reduce the appearance of fine lines" / "Promotes a smoother-looking complexion" Clinical study measuring fine line appearance
