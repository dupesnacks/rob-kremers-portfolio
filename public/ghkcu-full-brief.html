📋 Overview & Mission

Project: GHK-Cu Cosmetic Skincare Brand Validation

Objective: Evaluate and potentially launch a U.S. D2C or private-label GHK-Cu / Copper Tripeptide-1 cosmetic (cream/moisturizer/serum), positioned strictly as cosmetic — never drug/medical/injectable/peptide-therapy.

Target Price Band: $49–$69 retail
Brand Positioning: Premium-minimalist (Apple/Tesla restraint)
Timeline: 14 days (Jun 13 – Jul 14, 2026)
Status: Phase 1 🟡 In Progress

Success Outcomes (Pick One by Jul 14)

  • Outcome A (Go): Credible supplier + samples + compliant claims + insurance + working unit economics + real customer interest
  • Outcome B (Pivot): Adjacent lower-risk opportunity (content/community, GLP-1 lifestyle brand, bundles, affiliate, tooling)
  • Outcome C (No-Go): Any No-Go Trigger fires → decision to pause or kill

Why GHK-Cu Now?

  • Copper tripeptides have proven skincare efficacy (published research)
  • Positioning as cosmetic (not drug/therapeutic) avoids FDA classification burden
  • Growing awareness in skincare community; white space for premium cosmetic positioning
  • Lean MVP possible: $15–25k inventory, $5–10k marketing to validate
  • Three clear outcomes: go/pivot/no-go within 14 days allows for quick pivots

🛑 Claims Guardrails (FTC/FDA Compliance)

Safe (Cosmetic) Claims — Use Freely

  • ✓ Helps skin look smoother
  • ✓ Improves the appearance of fine lines
  • ✓ Moisturizes and supports a youthful-looking complexion
  • ✓ Helps skin feel soft, hydrated, refreshed
  • ✓ Supports healthy-looking skin
  • ✓ Improves the look of skin texture
  • ✓ Lightweight hydrating copper peptide serum

Prohibited (Drug/Medical) Claims — Never Use

  • ✗ Stimulates collagen (biological mechanism claim)
  • ✗ Repairs/regenerates skin (tissue repair = drug definition)
  • ✗ Heals wounds (medical claim)
  • ✗ Treats scars, acne, eczema, rosacea, inflammation (disease/condition claims)
  • ✗ Reduces inflammation (systemic effect claim)
  • ✗ Reverses aging (anti-aging benefit claim)
  • ✗ Rebuilds tissue (tissue repair = drug definition)
  • ✗ Medical-grade peptide therapy (therapy language = drug framing)
  • ✗ "Ozempic face" treatment (disease/condition treatment)
  • ✗ Tightens loose skin after weight loss (tissue tightening = mechanism)
  • ✗ Any injectable/research peptide framing (positions as pharmaceutical)

Enforcement Rule

If any draft (landing page, Amazon listing, influencer brief, ad) contains a Prohibited claim:

  1. OpenClaw rewrites it immediately using Safe alternatives
  2. Flag violation in DECISIONS.md with date and context
  3. Never send violating version for review without explicit approval to pivot to drug positioning
Legal Review Needed (Phase 1): Attorney must confirm Safe claims are substantiable and Prohibited claims are correctly identified. Current status: Attorney review scheduled for Jun 20.
