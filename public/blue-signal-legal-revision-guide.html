🔴 Issues Found in Original Documents

Issue 1: "Stimulates Collagen" (DRUG CLAIM)

❌ In original doc:
"Multi-functional: collagen stimulation + anti-inflammatory + skin barrier repair"

Why it's a problem: Saying a product "stimulates" anything = affecting body structure/function = DRUG CLAIM (illegal for cosmetics)

FTC Risk: Warning letter, forced corrective advertising ($50k+), fines
✅ Legal Version:
"Appearance-focused: helps skin look firmer + promotes healthier-looking complexion + supports skin's natural appearance"

Issue 2: "Regenerate" & "Repair" (THERAPEUTIC CLAIMS)

❌ In original doc:
"Clinically proven to repair, regenerate, and restore healthy skin"

Why it's a problem: "Repair," "regenerate," "restore" = therapeutic intent (fixing damaged tissue) = DRUG CLAIM
✅ Legal Version:
"Backed by published research showing it may help skin appear healthier, more resilient, and more youthful-looking"

Issue 3: "Reduce Inflammation" (DRUG CLAIM)

❌ In original doc:
"Collagen stimulation + inflammation + skin barrier repair"

Why it's a problem: Reducing inflammation = treating a medical symptom = DRUG CLAIM
✅ Legal Version:
"Helps skin appear calmer and more even-looking"

Issue 4: Absolute Claims Without Disclaimers

❌ In original doc:
"GHK-Cu is proven to..." (no hedge language, no disclaimer)

Why it's a problem: Absolute claims without substantiation + no "results vary" disclaimer = FTC violation
✅ Legal Version:
"Published research suggests GHK-Cu may help skin appear..." + "Results vary by individual" + "For cosmetic use only"
