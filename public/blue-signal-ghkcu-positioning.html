A: GHK-Cu is a copper tripeptide—a naturally occurring molecule in your body that declines with age. It signals your skin to produce collagen, reduce inflammation, and repair itself. We've formulated it at clinical-grade concentrations to deliver measurable results.
Q: Why GHK-Cu over retinol or vitamin C?
A: They're different tools. Retinol is aggressive (great for some, irritating for others). Vitamin C is antioxidant support. GHK-Cu is regenerative—it tells your skin to repair itself. Ideal if you have sensitivity, aging concerns, or a compromised barrier. Many dermatologists use all three, but GHK-Cu is often the gentler entry point.
